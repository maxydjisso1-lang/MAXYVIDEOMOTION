# storytelling

## Purpose
This skill is pure knowledge, with no CLI. It gives creative-director proven structures and platform norms so that plans are deliberate rather than generic.

## Inputs
The objective, platform, duration, available material (transcript and shots) and brand tone.

## Outputs
A recommendation, meaning a structure with section roles and time budgets plus hook options. It feeds into `creative-plan.json`.

## Tools
None. Use this reference.

## Structures (duration budget as a % of total)
| Structure | Sections | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| hook-problem-solution-cta | hook 10 · problem 20 · solution 40 · proof 15 · cta 15 | conversion ads |
| aida | attention 10 · interest 30 · desire 40 · action 20 | product launches |
| pas | problem 25 · agitate 25 · solution 35 · cta 15 | pain-driven offers |
| story-arc | hook 10 · setup 25 · turn 30 · resolution 25 · cta 10 | brand films, founders |
| listicle | hook 10 · items 75 (N equal) · cta 15 | education, tips |
| demo | result-first hook 15 · steps 65 · cta 20 | product and features |
| testimonial | quote hook 15 · before 25 · after 40 · cta 20 | social proof |
| teaser | reveal fragments 80 · logo/date 20 | launches |

## Hook techniques (first 1–3 s)
- **bold-claim:** a strong statement.
- **question:** only if the answer arrives within 5 s.
- **result-first:** show the outcome, then the how.
- **pattern-interrupt:** an unexpected visual or sound.
- **stat:** use the Statistic motion component.
- **visual-reveal:** open on the product.
- **quote:** the strongest line from a testimonial.

Rules: no logo intro before the hook on social (it goes in the outro). The hook must make sense with the sound off, so on-screen text or captions are mandatory.

## Platform norms
| Platform | Ideal length | Pace (avg shot) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 15–35 s | 1.5–3 s | captions on; the first frame is the thumbnail; CTA verbal + visual |
| Instagram feed 1:1 / 4:5 | 15–60 s | 2–4 s | sound often off |
| YouTube 16:9 | 1–10 min | 3–8 s | a slower hook is OK; chapters |
| LinkedIn | 30–90 s | 3–5 s | professional tone, captions essential |
| Advertising (broadcast) | 6 / 15 / 20 / 30 s exact | brand-driven | exact duration, −23 LUFS |

## CTA writing
Use a verb and a benefit in 40 characters or fewer. Put one CTA only. Match `brand.tone`. Examples: "Découvrir la collection", "Essayez gratuitement 14 jours", "Book your demo".

## Constraints
These are guidelines, not laws. The brand and the brief win. Always explain any deviation in the plan `rationale`.

## Examples
- A 30 s Reels ad from a founder interview uses story-arc, compressed to hook (3 s) → setup (7 s) → turn (9 s) → resolution (7 s) → CTA (4 s).
- A 45 s LinkedIn tips video uses a listicle with 3 items of about 11 s each, a stat hook and a calm pace.

## Failure handling
If no structure fits the material, pick `custom`, state the section roles explicitly, and justify the choice in `rationale`.
